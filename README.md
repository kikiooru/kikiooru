Q: What is the difference between a villain and a antihero?

A: A villain is the antagonist of the episode/chapter, acting by egoism, revenge, purely wicked. Don't cares about wo will hurt to win. Represents evil. A good example is Voldemort from the Harry Potter books and movies
A antihero is the protagonist, being moraly questionable. They may be egoistic or violent, but do the good thing at the end. People tend to empathize with the character because seems more realistic and human than the villain. We can hold Deadpool from Marvel as an example.
